Each detector is calibrated separately; its error estimate is the spread of its cross-validation committee. Ensemble weights are learned by a meta-classifier from validation data, never fixed by hand.

Similarity analysis is separate from AI detection. It compares the text with your local reference corpus (exact phrases, n-grams, document and sentence similarity). Overlap is not evidence of AI generation, and AI likelihood is not evidence of copying.
